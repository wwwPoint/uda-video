Rule 4: Mark the danger
"From a safe distance, you can also place a sign or marker — for example, using stones — so that other people know this area is dangerous."

In a mined area
"If you find yourself in a mined area, for example, on a minefield, stop, calm down, and warn others not to move toward you. Call for help, but be sure to emphasize that no one should approach you."
Speaker 00:55 - 01:21
Witness to an explosion
"If you witness a mine detonation or another incident, stop, calm down, and do not approach the victim. Remain at a safe distance, speak with the victim, keep them calm, and if possible, ask them to stop the bleeding themselves. Call 112 immediately, wait for emergency responders, and keep talking to the victim."
