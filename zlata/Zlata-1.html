Speaker 00:05 - 00:13
What is Mine Safety?
"Do you know what mine safety is? Mine safety is a set of rules that save lives if you encounter explosive hazards. Today, in our reality, we may come across various types of them."
Speaker 00:14 - 00:22
1. Unexploded Ordnance (UXO)
"First, unexploded ammunition and ordnance. They have no shelf life and can detonate with the slightest contact. Therefore, collecting scrap metal or military vehicle remnants is strictly forbidden."
Speaker 00:23 - 00:28
2. Drones & UAV Wreckage
"Second, UAVs and drones. You must not touch or disassemble their wreckage, because drones can drop targeted mines or carry explosive charges prior to crashing."
Speaker 00:29 - 00:35
3. Falling Bullets & Ammo
"Third, stray bullets fired at aerial targets that fall back to the ground at high speeds. Therefore, during shelling, do not stand near windows or pick up live ammunition."
Speaker 00:36 - 00:55
4. Mines, Cluster Munitions & Traps
"Special attention must be paid to mines:
  • Anti-personnel mines: like the inconspicuous plastic 'petal' (butterfly) mine.
  • Anti-vehicle mines: which are typically disguised with anti-personnel mines nearby.
  • Cluster submunitions: small scattered bomblets with touch triggers.
  • Naval & river mines: making swimming strictly forbidden in mined waters.
  • Tripwires & disguised booby traps: hidden under discarded backpacks, boxes, toys, or notebooks.
